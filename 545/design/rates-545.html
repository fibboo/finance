#545 · Exchange rate in transaction forms

Only the hint under the second amount changes: the amount received in a transfer and the original amount of an expense or income paid in another currency. The equation always names both currencies, and the direction is chosen so the right-hand number is at least 1. The valuation hint under the amount (≈ $1,119.07 · rate 106.4461) and everything outside the forms stay as they are. Dashed outlines mark what changes.
