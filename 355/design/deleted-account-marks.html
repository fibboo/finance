Deleted accounts in transactions

A deleted account's name is muted and struck through wherever a transaction shows it: in the rows of the Transactions feed and of the recent transactions of Home, in the title of the transaction details and in its postings. Screen readers hear “deleted” after the name.

  • In the postings, an active money account is a link with ›, which opens that account; a deleted one is never a link.
  • Only active money accounts link: categories, income sources and Equity have no screen yet.
  • Home opens the account beside the transaction; the Transactions section leads to the account in Accounts.
