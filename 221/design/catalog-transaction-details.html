Transaction details beside a catalog list

Desktop only. Agreed: variant A, three columns. A row of the feed is a link: it opens the details of the transaction with its account beside the feed, and the first transaction is shown while none is chosen. The Transactions icon leaves the rows on desktop, since All in the block opens the same list. The main menu collapses to icons. A phone does not change.

1 · The menu collapsed by itself

At 1280 px the three columns and the full menu do not fit together, so the menu shows icons only: the logo mark, Add transaction as a plus, the six sections and, at the bottom, the button that shows the full menu. The freed width goes to the list, and the feed keeps its 26 rem.

2 · The full menu on a wide screen

At 1440 px everything fits beside the full menu. Collapse at its bottom turns it into the icons by hand; the choice is remembered.

3 · The full menu shown where it does not fit

The button of the collapsed menu opens the full one over the page, without moving the columns. It closes on Collapse, on a click outside it and after a section is chosen.

4 · Locations and Income sources

The same three columns. A location shows the expenses made there, an income source its incomes; the account under the transaction is the money account of that transaction.

Rules

  • Where the menu collapses by itself. On every page, below the width at which the page's own columns fit beside the full menu; on Categories that is about 1400 px. A menu collapsed or opened by hand stays so on a wide screen.
  • Narrow desktop. Between 1024 px and about 1130 px three columns do not fit even beside the icons. There the details column is left out and the feed rows are not links, as today.
  • The account panel. The account search param already holds the chosen entry, so the panel always shows the money account of the shown transaction. A link to an account inside the transaction details opens the Accounts section.
