Categories, Locations and Income sources

Three new sections built like Accounts: a searchable list with the details beside it on desktop and in a bottom sheet on a phone. Variants to choose from are marked A and B. Base currency USD.

1 · Navigation on a phone: More

Agreed: Add transaction floats as a round button above the bar on the right, and the bar holds Home, Transactions, Accounts, Categories and More. More opens a menu with Locations and Income sources and is marked as current while one of them is open. The desktop sidebar lists all six sections in the same order and keeps its Add transaction button (section 2).

2 · Categories: filters

Agreed: the type segment and the Deleted toggle sit in one row under the search, the toggle right after the segment. It is on by default: deleted entries show among the active ones, struck through, with Restore as their one button. The list is grouped by type, like Assets and Liabilities in Accounts; a chosen type leaves one group. The block on the right is a feed as tall as the list: it runs to the bottom of the page and scrolls inside, loading further pages.

3 · Categories on a phone

No details and no recent transactions on a phone: the row is not clickable, and its buttons do everything.

4 · Deleted off

With the toggle off the list holds active entries only. A restore refused because an active entry took the name in the meantime shows the error notification.

5 · Locations and Income sources

The same section without a type filter. Transactions gets a Locations filter chip after Income, built like the Categories one, so a location can open its transactions too.

6 · Details opened from a transaction

In the details of a transaction the category, the income source and the location become links, as a money account is today. Each opens its own details, in the details column on desktop and in a bottom sheet on a phone: Transactions, Edit and Delete, or Transactions and Restore for a deleted one.

What else changes

  • No amounts in the rows. The API's balance of a category or an income source is its all-time total, so the rows show the name and the description only.
  • Delete has no blocking reason for these entries: the backend deletes a category, an income source or a location whatever its transactions. It asks for confirmation in the shared DeleteConfirmation.
  • No details panel in the sections. It would repeat the row; on desktop the right side shows the transactions of the chosen entry instead, which a phone leaves out. The details exist only where they are opened from a transaction (section 6).
  • The Categories filter chip of Transactions and the pickers keep listing active entries only.
