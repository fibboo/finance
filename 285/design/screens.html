Edit a transaction

Edit sits at the bottom of a transaction's details, beside Delete, and opens the form of its type, filled in, under the title Edit <type> and without the type chips. A balance adjustment has no Edit. Base currency USD in every board.

1 · Details on a phone

Agreed: D2. Edit and Delete share the bottom row as two equal icon tiles, in the transaction's details and in the account's.

2 · Transactions on desktop

The same details in the column on the right.

3 · The edit form

The add form of the type, filled with the transaction's values. The title names the type, the type chips are gone, and the button reads Save changes. Pickers list only accounts of the same kind, so the type cannot change.

What changes

  • Edit shows in the details of an expense, an income and a transfer; an adjustment keeps only Delete, as a full-width trash tile.
  • Delete becomes a trash icon in the danger color, in the transaction's and the account's details; a blocking hint stays above the row.
  • The account's Edit moves from the top row to the bottom one, beside Delete.
  • Saving closes the form, keeps the transaction's details open and refreshes the list, the summary and the account balances.
  • A refusal shows at its field or above Save changes, as in the add form; the form stays open.
