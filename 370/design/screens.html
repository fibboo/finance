What changes besides the layout

  • Details are always open on desktop in Transactions, Accounts and Recent on Home: with nothing chosen, the top row's details show, without a close button. A phone opens nothing until a row is tapped.
  • Currency always shows on the second line, also for USD accounts (as the Accounts list does: Wallet · USD); the rate shows only outside USD.
  • A transfer's title becomes Transfer, and From → To moves to the second line, so the title is never cut by long account names.
  • Rows grow from 32 to 44 px on a phone and from 36 to 52 px on desktop, so a screen holds fewer of them; Recent on Home gets the same rows.
